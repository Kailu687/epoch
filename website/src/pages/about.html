About Epoch

Epoch aims to make machine learning training accessible without hiding the parts that make results trustworthy.

Mission

Provide a local-first training platform that handles data inspection, evaluation protocol and reproducibility correctly by default, with expert controls for people who want them.

Who it is for

  • Analysts who have a spreadsheet and a question.
  • Engineers who need reproducible baselines and exportable pipelines.
  • Researchers who need protocol-correct evaluation.

Status

The project is in early development. See the roadmap for what exists.
